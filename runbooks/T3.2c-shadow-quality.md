Read `runbooks/_wave5-common.md` first. Report: `reports/T3.2c.md`.

still uncommitted) compiles with zero shader errors at ~59 fps. BUT the shadows in the screenshot
are still blocky and pixelated, like before. Goal: find out why and fix it for the
Emscripten/WebGL2 build only.

`upstream/source/renderer/backend/gl/ShaderProgram.cpp`, `upstream/source/renderer/backend/gl/Texture.cpp`,

compare mode or LINEAR filter on the depth texture, a smaller map size, PCF switched off). Keep each
