**Observed summary**: with `pbl=0` the page survived the whole 800 s scenario and produced 12 clean
60 fps windows; with PBL on (default) the page went silent 12 s after load, before the menu, and
never recovered in 10+ minutes. **Inferred** (not proven by this measurement): PBL is implicated in
A's freeze, but A's freeze is an *early startup* freeze, not the mid-match freeze at 345 s that
T0.4g saw — so this runbook does **not** show the mid-match stall reproducing, and the two freezes
may not be the same defect. I did not guess a fix and did not retry (the runbook allows a retry
only for obviously transient errors; a busy-but-silent renderer is not).
