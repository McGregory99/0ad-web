- **Observed:** the `#version` line is not generated by the engine — it comes from the shader source itself (`#version 110`/`#version 120`), and the GLES branch in `ShaderProgram.cpp:723-726` rewrites it to `#version 100` plus `precision highp float;` for fragment shaders. - **Observed:** a GLES path for the shadow sampler exists: `SceneRenderer.cpp:235` sets `useShadowSampler{true}` for GLES and adds `USE_SHADOW_SAMPLER` (line 238); `common/texture.h` contains `shadow2D` (Step 3: shadow2D=1) and `Texture.cpp:323-329` sets `GL_TEXTURE_COMPARE_MODE = GL_COMPARE_R_TO_TEXTURE` with `GL_TEXTURE_COMPARE_FUNC`. That combination is exactly what WebGL2 cannot provide under GLSL ES 1.00 — hence blocky shadows.
- **Derived:** of the 82 shader files, only **5** contain GLSL ES 1.00-only constructs: `common/vertex.h` (attribute), `common/stage.h` (varying), `common/texture.h` (texture2D, textureCube, shadow2D), `common/fragment.h` (gl_FragColor, gl_FragData), `model_water.fs` (texture2D). 77 files are clean.
- **Derived:** the most common legacy constructs are `texture2D` and `gl_FragData` (2 occurrences each), followed by `attribute`, `varying`, `textureCube`, `gl_FragColor`, `shadow2D` (1 each). `#extension` appears 10 times, 9 of them in `ffx_a.h`.
